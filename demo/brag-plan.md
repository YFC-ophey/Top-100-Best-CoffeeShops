# Brag Plan: ROAST.

## What is this app?
ROAST. is an interactive world-map explorer for *The World's 100 Best Coffee Shops* — 200 ranked shops across 56 countries (the Top 100 Global + Top 100 South America lists), with country-level density bubbles that drill down to individual shops and one-tap Google Maps directions. It runs at zero cost and needs no API key from visitors.

## The angle
A playful, good-vibes "coffee-lover's map reveal." The premise is the founder's real promise from the README: *wherever the coffee vibes hit, open ROAST. and instantly see if one of the world's best coffee shops is near you.* The video earns its specificity from real numbers (200 shops, 56 countries), the real #1 (Onyx Coffee Lab), and the actual dark-roast UI — not generic map-app filler. It's warm and postable, with a happy little flex at the end: the whole thing is free and refreshes itself every February.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Espresso-dark screen. A single gold coffee-bean icon glows on, then the line slams in: **"Is one of the world's best coffee shops near you?"** Hold it. That question is the entire reason to keep watching.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The world map igniting — orange country-density bubbles pop in one continent at a time, with a counter settling on **200 shops · 56 countries**.
- A tap on the **Peru** bubble (28 shops — the densest) zooms the map in; individual shop markers pulse to life.
- The shop **detail panel** slides in: hero photo, **#1 Top 100 · Onyx Coffee Lab** rank badge, a bright **Get Directions** button, and the Collection / Country / Rank Band filter chips.

## Outro / punchline
Clean card: **"Free. No API key. Auto-updated every February."** Then the ROAST. wordmark (orange dot) with the tagline **"Wherever the coffee vibes hit."** and the live URL.

## User flow worth showing
entry → key action → result:
1. **Entry** — the global map with country density bubbles.
2. **Key action** — tap a country bubble; map zooms to individual shop markers.
3. **Result** — a shop's detail panel with rank, address, and Get Directions.
This is the centerpiece. Scenes 2-4 are this flow.

## Tone
- Preset: default
- Creative direction: playful, good-vibes coffee-lover map reveal — warm and postable, café-at-golden-hour energy without the corporate polish
- Interpretation: comfortable pacing with room for each moment to breathe (4-5 scenes); friendly, conversational copy; clean crossfade/wipe transitions and lively motion. Warm and fun, not premium-restrained — but still clean, never chaotic.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20s

- Background: `#130a08` (espresso dark; radial orange/gold glows over a `158deg` gradient to `#21120d`)
- Accent: `#df6e2e` (brand orange) with `#f7c69a` gold for highlights
- Text: `#f8efe6` (warm white); muted `#b8a08f`
- Display font: Cabinet Grotesk (700/800)
- Body font: General Sans (400-600)
- Strongest visual element: the dark glassmorphism map panel with glowing orange country-density bubbles and the pulsing shop markers (`pulse-ring` animation); secondary: the shop detail card with the `#1 Top 100` rank badge.

## Share copy (draft)
Built ROAST. — an interactive map of the world's 100 best coffee shops (200 of them, 56 countries). Free, no API key, and it finds the nearest great cup wherever you are. ☕

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed — gentle, premium, café-at-golden-hour
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (~115 BPM, warm, upbeat-but-soft)
- Music treatment: start at 0s under the hook at moderate-low volume, let it open up as the map ignites, soft fade-out under the outro card.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset for vol-9. Target strong cues — **4.23s** and **6.34s** for the map/bubble ignition, **10.54s** for the country-tap zoom, **12.65s** for the detail-panel slide-in. Beat grid ~0.52s spacing for bubble pop sequence; for any sequential bubbles hold the full set after.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — let bass/RMS make the map glow and bubbles breathe slightly; no waveform bars.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched, professional. Soft UI pops on bubble reveals, a gentle interface click on the country tap, a clean slide/whoosh on the detail panel, one soft chime when the rank badge settles.
- Audio-coupled moments: continent-by-continent bubble pops; the count-up to 200/56; simulated tap on the Peru bubble; the Get Directions / rank-badge settle.
- Restraint rule: audio must never get loud, busy, or comedic; no harsh stingers. Cozy and confident only.

The dark glass map panel fills the frame. Orange country-density bubbles pop in continent by continent (South America densest, then Europe, North America, Asia). A counter ticks up and settles: **200 shops · 56 countries · 2 lists**. Legend chip reads "Country bubble."

Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Tap a country — 4s
Cursor moves to the **Peru** bubble (label "Peru · 28 shops") and taps. The map zooms in; the country bubble dissolves into individual shop markers that pulse (the real `pulse-ring` glow). Warm white shop dots over the dark map.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulate a cursor tap on the Peru bubble, then markers pulse in

Audio-coupled idea: gentle interface click on tap; soft swell as markers pulse, aligned to strong cue ~10.54s
Music: warm, present
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 4
